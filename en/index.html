Privacy Policy

Ayali app · Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: all your data stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics, and we do not collect or transmit any information about you or your children.

1. Who we are

Ayali helps parents keep their children's health and growth record (vaccines, growth, medications, illnesses, documents…) and get reminders. Developer: Mohamed Magdy. See "Contact" below.

2. Data you enter

The app stores what you type or choose, which may include:

  • Child details: name, date of birth, sex, blood type, birth details, the child's doctor's name and phone.
  • Health records: vaccines, weight/height/head circumference, medications and doses, allergies, illnesses and temperature readings, doctor visits, developmental milestones.
  • Daily log: feeding, sleep and diapers.
  • Documents: photos (camera or gallery) or PDF files you add, with expiry dates and notes.
  • Moments album: photos of your child with a date and an optional caption.

This is children's health data and we treat it with the highest care.

3. Where it is stored

  • On your device only. We operate no server and there is no cloud sync.
  • The database is encrypted with a random key kept in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain; it never leaves the device.
  • Document files live in the app's private storage (not in your photo gallery), protected by the operating system.

4. What we do not do

  • No account creation or sign-in.
  • No ads, ad SDKs or tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools that send data anywhere.
  • The app needs no internet connection and talks to no server.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Maps and calling

The "Nearby right now" buttons (hospital / pharmacy / pediatrician) open your maps app with a search word only (e.g. "pharmacy"); the maps app itself uses your location with your permission. Ayali never requests location permission and never knows where you are. The ambulance button opens the dialer with 123.

Home-screen widget (optional)

If you add the widget it shows your child's name and the next vaccine on the home screen, without the app lock. Don't add it if you don't want that.

6. Sharing and backups

The app never sends anything by itself. You may choose to share:

  • The emergency card as an image, the doctor report as a PDF (it can include album photos), or a document file.
  • Sharing with a partner: a passphrase-encrypted file you send by any means; your partner merges it into their app. There is no server in between.
  • An encrypted backup (AES-256) protected by a passphrase you choose, saved or sent wherever you decide (WhatsApp, Drive, etc.). If you forget the passphrase we cannot open the backup.

Anything you share leaves the app to the destination you chose and is then subject to that service's policy.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app: More → Child profile → Delete child and all data, or Settings → Erase all data. This permanently removes the data and files from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data.
  • Backups you saved yourself are yours to delete from wherever you stored them.

8. Children's privacy

Ayali is intended for parents and guardians, not for children, and contains nothing aimed at children. The parent or guardian enters and controls the child's data.

9. Medical disclaimer

Ayali is a recording, organizing and reminder tool, not a substitute for your child's doctor. It provides no diagnosis, treatment or dose calculation. Reference content (vaccine schedule, growth curves, milestones) is taken from the sources named in the app and is reviewed with a pediatrician. In an emergency call your local emergency number (123 in Egypt) or your child's doctor.

10. Your rights

All your data is in your hands on your device: you can view, edit, delete and export it (via backup) at any time. This is consistent with data-subject rights under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

11. Changes

If we ever add a feature that changes how data is handled (such as optional cloud sync) we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent before any upload. The "last updated" date is shown above.

12. Contact

For any privacy question, open an issue on GitHub.
